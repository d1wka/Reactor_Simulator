SAFETY SYSTEMS
VVER-1200 — pressurized water reactor, 3200 MW thermal / 1200 MW electric. Primary coolant at 157 bar remains liquid above 325°C. Hover over any reactor component for detailed information.
Thermodynamic cycle: Fission heat → primary coolant (loop 1) → steam generator (heat exchanger) → secondary steam loop → turbine → generator → condenser → feedwater pump → steam generator. Two isolated loops prevent radioactive contamination of the turbine.
Nuclear fission: ²³⁵U absorbs a slow (thermal) neutron → unstable ²³⁶U → splits into two fission fragments + 2–3 fast neutrons + ~200 MeV energy. Water moderator slows neutrons (eV range). Control rods (B₄C/Hf) absorb neutrons to regulate chain reaction. keff = 1.000 at criticality.
Defence-in-depth: 1) Fuel ceramic matrix · 2) Zircaloy cladding · 3) Reactor pressure vessel (steel, 30 cm) · 4) Primary circuit · 5) Containment building (prestressed concrete, 1.2 m). ECCS, passive core flood tanks, emergency boration system, and auto SCRAM protect against all design-basis accidents.
